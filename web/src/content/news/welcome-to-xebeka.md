Xebeka is an online role-playing game you play in the browser, on a computer or a phone. There is nothing to install.

You begin on an island of your own, where you can build a forge, a storage chest and an alchemy tower. From there you cross to Bonetown to trade and gear up. In his cellar in Bonetown, the Time Sage hands out errands that lead into old legends, each with its own boss and relic. Beyond the town lie the wild isles, home to goblins, orcs, minotaurs and worse.

An account holds up to five characters, and each one keeps its own save. Leave the game on your computer and carry on from your phone, right where you stopped.

The first world is called Pandora. It opens soon, as a beta that will keep growing. The news of the opening will appear here first.
